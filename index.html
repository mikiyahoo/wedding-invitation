Our Love Story

How it began
A Hello on Telegram

It started with a single message. One hello turned into long conversations that ran late into the night, and somewhere between the typing and the waiting, a name on a screen became the best part of the day.

Two years together
From Strangers to Sure

Two years of shared meals, long walks and ordinary afternoons that never felt ordinary. We learned each other’s stories, habits and dreams — and curiosity quietly grew into certainty.

The decision
Two Families, One Promise

We brought our families together, and with their blessing we chose the rest of our lives. What began with one message now becomes a marriage — and we would love for you to be there when it does.
